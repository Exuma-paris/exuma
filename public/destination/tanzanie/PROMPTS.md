# Image prompts — Tanzanie

All images go in `/public/destination/tanzanie/`. Format: PNG. Run `gen-images.py` to produce them from the reference photos in `references/destination/tanzanie/`.

## Hero images

### `hero-1.png`
**Reference search:** "Serengeti plains golden hour wildebeest Tanzania"
**Prompt:** Wide panoramic view of the Serengeti plains at golden hour, large herd of wildebeest silhouetted against a warm amber sky, flat acacia-dotted savanna stretching to the horizon, no people, photorealistic, cinematic.

### `hero-2.png`
**Reference search:** "Ngorongoro crater aerial view Tanzania volcanic caldera"
**Prompt:** Aerial perspective from the rim of the Ngorongoro caldera looking down into the vast flat crater floor, lush green edges transitioning to golden savanna below, small water bodies visible, hazy morning light, photorealistic.

### `hero-3.png`
**Reference search:** "Mahale chimpanzee forest Tanzania"
**Prompt:** Close-up chimpanzee in dense tropical forest, dappled light through canopy, lush green vegetation, Lake Tanganyika visible in background through trees, no people, photorealistic.
