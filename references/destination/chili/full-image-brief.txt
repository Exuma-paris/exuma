Subject: a small band of wild guanacos (Lama guanicoe) in Torres del Paine National Park, Chilean Patagonia — an original wide panoramic landscape frame.

Animals: three or four guanacos, anatomically exact — slender long neck, small wedge-shaped head with large dark eyes and short erect ears, cinnamon-fawn upper body, clean white underbelly, chest and inner legs, grey-fawn face, long thin legs, short tail. Wild camelids, NOT llamas or alpacas: no wool tufts, no halters, no ornaments. One adult stands alert in profile with its head raised toward the mountains; the others graze in the golden grass a few metres behind, at different distances, in a loose natural spread — not lined up, not centred, not symmetrical.

Setting: the near foreground is the low gravel shore of a glacial river braid, its water a milky turquoise. The middle ground is open Patagonian pampa — dry ochre and straw-coloured coirón tussock grass, low thorny shrubs, a few bleached bare lenga trunks left standing. Behind it, the dark grey-black granite and shale of the Paine massif rises with snowfields in its couloirs, and the three granite towers of the Torres stand set back to one side of the frame, partly hazed by distance, not dead centre.

Light and weather: late afternoon, roughly an hour before sunset. Low warm raking side light that skims the grass and grazes the animals' flanks, long soft shadows on the pampa, the granite walls half in shade. A high sky brushed with fine wind-drawn cirrus, some cloud caught on the summits. The Patagonian wind visible in the leaning grass and in a light ripple on the water.

Camera: eye level, standing on the shore — never aerial or drone. Long lens character, around 200mm, shot from a respectful distance; the mountains read large and compressed behind the animals. Deep enough focus that the massif stays legible, with the very near foreground grass slightly soft. Wide panoramic composition built for a full-width web banner: the horizon low, generous sky, the animals placed off-centre on the lower third.

No people, no vehicles, no fences, no buildings, no signage.
